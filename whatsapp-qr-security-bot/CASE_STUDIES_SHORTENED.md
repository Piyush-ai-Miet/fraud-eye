### Case 1: Ramlal Sharma - ₹2.3 Lakh UPI QR Scam
**Hindi:**
```
Jaipur के किराना दुकानदार रामलाल को WhatsApp पर QR code मिला - "LPG Subsidy ₹5000"। 
उन्होंने सोचा पैसे मिलेंगे और QR scan कर दिया। लेकिन वह payment request था - ₹2.3 lakh account से कट गए। 
Police ने बताया यह fake government scheme scam है।
```

**English:**
```
Grocery shop owner Ramlal from Jaipur received a QR code on WhatsApp - "LPG Subsidy ₹5000". 
He thought he would receive money and scanned the QR. But it was a payment request - ₹2.3 lakh was deducted from his account. 
Police said it's a fake government scheme scam.
```

**Hinglish:**
```
Jaipur ke kirana dukaandaar Ramlal ko WhatsApp par QR code mila - "LPG Subsidy ₹5000". 
Unhone socha paise milenge aur QR scan kar diya. Lekin woh payment request tha - ₹2.3 lakh account se kat gaye. 
Police ne bataya yeh fake government scheme scam hai.
```

### Case 2: Sunita Devi - ₹4.8 Lakh AI Voice Scam
**Hindi:**
```
Lucknow की retired teacher सुनीता देवी को phone आया - caller ने खुद को "CBI Officer" बताया। 
Voice बिल्कुल असली लग रही थी। 6 घंटे तक डराया "money laundering case है, arrest हो जाएगी"। 
डर में उन्होंने ₹4.8 lakh transfer कर दिए। बाद में पता चला - यह AI deepfake voice थी, असली CBI officer नहीं।
```

**English:**
```
Retired teacher Sunita Devi from Lucknow received a phone call - caller claimed to be "CBI Officer". 
Voice sounded completely real. Threatened for 6 hours "money laundering case, you'll be arrested". 
In fear, she transferred ₹4.8 lakh. Later discovered - it was AI deepfake voice, not a real CBI officer.
```

**Hinglish:**
```
Lucknow ki retired teacher Sunita Devi ko phone aaya - caller ne khud ko "CBI Officer" bataya. 
Voice bilkul asli lag rahi thi. 6 ghante tak daraya "money laundering case hai, arrest ho jayegi". 
Dar mein unhone ₹4.8 lakh transfer kar diye. Baad mein pata chala - yeh AI deepfake voice thi, asli CBI officer nahi.
```

### Case 3: Prem Singh - ₹3.2 Lakh KYC Phishing Scam
**Hindi:**
```
Indore के किसान प्रेम सिंह को SMS आया - "SBI KYC pending, 24 घंटे में update करें"। 
Link पर click किया तो fake website खुली - बिल्कुल असली SBI जैसी दिख रही थी। 
उन्होंने account details और OTP भर दिया। 2 घंटे में ₹3.2 lakh - जो फसल बेचकर जमा किए थे - पूरे खाली हो गए।
```

**English:**
```
Farmer Prem Singh from Indore received SMS - "SBI KYC pending, update within 24 hours". 
Clicked the link and a fake website opened - looked exactly like real SBI. 
He filled account details and OTP. Within 2 hours, ₹3.2 lakh - which he deposited after selling crops - was completely emptied.
```

**Hinglish:**
```
Indore ke kisaan Prem Singh ko SMS aaya - "SBI KYC pending, 24 ghante mein update karo". 
Link par click kiya to fake website khuli - bilkul asli SBI jaisi dikh rahi thi. 
Unhone account details aur OTP bhar diya. 2 ghante mein ₹3.2 lakh - jo fasal bechkar jama kiye the - pure khaali ho gaye.
```
